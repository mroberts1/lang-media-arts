13 topics:

While the arts have traditionally been divided into 6 categories  (architecture, sculpture, painting, literature/poetry, music, theatre/dance), with cinema as the seventh, the audiovisual media arts in the modern world comprise a dizzying multiplicity of  hybridized forms, including photography, radio, comics, television, print and TV advertising, videogames, music video, motion graphics, graphic novels, hyperfiction, video art, performance art, LARPing, stop-motion animation, motion capture, ambient media, projection mapping, immersive environments, podcasts, NFT art, animated GIFs, internet movies, drone choreography, escape rooms, and generative media.

This course sets out to map this complex field and to connect at least some of the proliferating nodes that comprise it into at least some intelligible formations, exploring its often intertwining genealogical threads, its expressive languages, and its aesthetics. The course is divided into four non-chronological sections: Ontologies, Anatomies, Affordances, Aesthetics
